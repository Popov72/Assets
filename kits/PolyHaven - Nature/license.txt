Poly Haven - Nature
===================

The assets in this directory are derived from assets published by Poly Haven:

    https://polyhaven.com/

Poly Haven assets are released under the CC0 1.0 Universal public-domain
dedication:

    https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The files in this directory are processed versions of the original assets and
are not distributed in their original form:

  - Textures were resized to 1024 x 1024 pixels.
  - Textures were compressed in KTX2 format.
  - High-poly geometry was decimated to better suit a real-time rendering
    pipeline.

Poly Haven did not produce or endorse these processed versions.
