Aquanova kit - mixed licensing
================================

This directory contains assets used by the Aquanova demo and editor. The
repository's default license does not apply uniformly to every file here.


Quaternius-derived wall modules
-------------------------------

The files under `Walls/` are modified pieces from the Modular SciFi MegaKit by
Quaternius:

    https://quaternius.com/packs/modularscifimegakit.html

The source pack is released under CC0 1.0 Universal:

    https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Attribution is not required, but is appreciated:

    Models by @Quaternius - https://www.patreon.com/quaternius


Aquanova project data and utility meshes
----------------------------------------

`collision.json`, `Props/AreaBox.glb`, and `Props/Player.glb` are project-
authored Aquanova data and utility geometry. They are distributed under the
Apache License 2.0 found at the repository root.


Restricted weapon models
------------------------

The item models under `Weapons/` were supplied for the Aquanova project. No
separate standalone redistribution or reuse license has been documented for
them. They are not CC0 and are not covered by the repository's Apache License
2.0. Treat them as project-only assets and do not reuse them outside Aquanova
without permission from their copyright holder.

This is the same restriction documented for the corresponding first-person
weapon models in Babylon-Lite's `lab/public/aquanova/ASSET-LICENSES.md`.
